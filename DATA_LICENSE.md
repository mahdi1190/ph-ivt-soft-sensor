## What this licence does NOT cover

| Material | Governed by |
|----------|-------------|
| Everything in `code/` and `notebooks/` | `LICENSE` (Academic and Research Use Licence) |
| Model outputs in `reports/` | `LICENSE` (ARUL) — Outputs of the Software, not data |
| Figures in `figures/` | `LICENSE` (ARUL) — generated by the Software |
| Fitted model parameters | `LICENSE` (ARUL) — part of the Software |
| The published article | © 2026 The Author(s), published by the Royal Society of Chemistry; CC BY-NC 4.0 |

The distinction matters. The files in `data/` are experimental measurements and
are open for any use with attribution. The files in `reports/` and `figures/`
are what the soft sensor produces, and are licensed on the same non-commercial
terms as the code that produces them.

Any model outputs committed to this repository are provided for convenience
only. `DATA.md` gives the exact commands to regenerate each of them from the
committed inputs.
